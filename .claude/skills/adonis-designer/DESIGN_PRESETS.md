# ADONISMOB15TH — Design Presets

Eight reusable directions. Each must use the real palette and typefaces
from `BRAND_SYSTEM.md` — a preset changes composition, lighting, and mood,
never the underlying brand system itself.

When asked for "options" (plural), pull from genuinely different presets
below — never produce three variations of the same one with the text moved.

---

## Preset 01 — Luxury Editorial

High-end fashion-campaign register. Asymmetric composition, generous
negative space, a single strong focal point (the product or a rose frame).
Cinzel used large and sparse — a word or two, not a paragraph. Gold appears
once, as a fine detail (a hairline rule, a single word), not a fill. Think
a magazine spread, not a flyer.

## Preset 02 — Dark Streetwear

Bold, urban, masculine, aggressive without losing sophistication. Tighter
crops, harder contrast between ink and bone, Cinzel at heavier weights
(700/900). This is the direction for "make it mean." Still restrained on
gold — aggression comes from contrast and scale, not from adding color.

## Preset 03 — Minimal Family

Clean, restrained, premium. The quietest preset. Centered composition,
maximum negative space, Cinzel at a lower weight (500/600), gold used only
in the wordmark. This is the preset for anything that should feel like a
private, dignified communication rather than a promotion.

## Preset 04 — Product Drop

Built to announce new clothing. The product photo is the hero — largest
element in the frame, minimal competing text (a date, a single word).
Rose (color) as a secondary, smaller accent, never overlapping the product
itself. Highest information density of any preset, but still uncluttered —
one primary message only ("New. [date].", not a paragraph).

## Preset 05 — Family Announcement

For official communications, not product marketing. No product photo.
Ink background, the omertà pattern as texture, wordmark and message in
bone/gold, Minimal Family's restraint but with the omertà texture doing
the visual work. Nothing here should read as a sales message.

## Preset 06 — Social Media

Optimized for Instagram, TikTok, Facebook — see `ASSET_GUIDE.md` for exact
dimensions. Slightly more forgiving on density than Luxury Editorial since
these get viewed small and fast, but the "no generic template" rule still
applies at full strength — this is the preset most likely to accidentally
drift toward looking like a template, so check `QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`
against it especially carefully.

## Preset 07 — Membership / Regalia

Focused on family identity and exclusive member products (anything sold
only to verified members, not the public). Most conservative preset —
maximum restraint, the wordmark and the rose (line variant, not color) as
the only visual elements besides the product. Should read as a private
seal, not an advertisement.

## Preset 08 — Campaign

For major clothing launches and promotions — the most ambitious preset,
closest to Luxury Editorial in production value but built for a coordinated
multi-asset rollout (a hero piece plus matching social/story variants).
Establish one strong composition here first, then adapt it to other formats
rather than designing each format from scratch.
